The studio model

Small enough to stay close. Built to reach globally. One connected team from the first thought to the final file.

Crab Studio is a group, not a single creative signature. Every discipline stays in conversation, so the work feels coherent at every touchpoint.

The structure expands around the brief, bringing filmmakers, photographers, writers, designers and specialist artists into one accountable team.

05Questions

Frequently asked.

Do you work with brands that already exist, or only from scratch?

Both. We can build a new identity from the ground up or work inside an established system, extending it through motion, film, social content and campaign design.

What makes Crab Studio different from a typical agency?

Strategy and hands-on execution stay in one room. Social, editing, motion, identity and content direction run as one workflow instead of being passed between disconnected teams.

Can you make 3D walkthroughs and films for a project that is not built yet?

Yes. Aurelle shows the full pipeline: a 3D model, a walkthrough, realistic stills, a brand film and the launch feed, all made before a single brick is laid.

Do you handle copywriting and content, or just design?

We shape content strategy, campaign ideas, scripts, messaging and the visual execution. The exact mix is agreed at the start so responsibilities stay clear.

What do you need from us before starting?

Your goals, audience, offer, current brand assets, channels, references, timeline and decision-makers. We help turn an incomplete brief into a focused plan.

What if we need changes after delivery?

We plan review rounds during production and can scope ongoing support after launch. For content partnerships, the system is designed to evolve with your calendar.
