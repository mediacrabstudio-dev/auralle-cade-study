(01)Process

Strategy

A calm coastal home for people who already have the city, and want distance from it. Everything else in the brand follows from that one idea.

The buyer

Who it is for.

Families from Mumbai who want a second home they can reach without leaving the city behind completely. They value privacy, space and light over spectacle, and they have seen every infinity-pool render already.

The idea

Quiet, not loud.

Most coastal launches shout: sunsets, slogans, offers. AURELLE does the opposite. Low buildings, deep shade, honest materials and a tone that never oversells. The line that carries it: a quieter kind of extraordinary.

The selling point

Mumbai, across the water.

From the deck, South Mumbai is a thin line on the horizon: close enough to know it is there, far enough to feel the difference. That single view became the anchor for the brochure, the film and the website.

Rules we set

What the brand never does.

  • No prices or unit countsNothing about availability, approvals or prices is stated as fact.
  • One call to actionEvery piece ends with a single, calm next step.
  • No stock clichésNo palm-silhouette sunset as the only idea.
  • Concept disclaimerEvery public piece says it is a design concept with visualised imagery.
