(02)Process

Identity

An arch standing on a horizon line: the doorway of the house and the edge of the sea. A palette taken from the site itself.

The mark

An arch, and the horizon.

A single open arch over one horizontal line. It works as a full lockup with the wordmark, as a small symbol on a gate light or a box, and as a favicon.

Colour

Taken from the site.

Copper is used once per layout: a rule, a dot, a hairline. Sage stays on lines and graphics because it is too light to carry text.

Type

A light serif, a clean sans.

Space to slow down.

A light display serif for everything you feel, a clean sans for everything you read. Italic replaces any script face.
