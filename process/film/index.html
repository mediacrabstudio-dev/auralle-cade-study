(05)Process

Film

A 58-second brand film, from the sea to the lit house at night. Camera moves come from the 3D model, so the film keeps the real architecture.

Structure

One continuous journey.

  • Sea to landA slow approach across the bay towards the estate.
  • Gate and courtyardThrough the timber gate, under the frangipani.
  • InsideLiving room, veranda and tea, the bedroom in morning light.
  • Pool and gardenThe afternoon by the water, a path to the shore.
  • NightThe aerial reveal: the house lit, the city across the water.
Process

Blocking first, then the film.

Every shot is planned as a Blender camera move on the real model. Those moves drive the video generation, which is why doors, columns and the pool stay where they belong from shot to shot.

Cutdowns

Built for the feed too.

The film is cut into a 23-second vertical reel and three square motion posts for the launch campaign.
