How it was made

From blockout to baked light.

  • BlockoutWalls, levels and openings drawn to the site-plan dimensions.
  • MaterialsPhoto-scanned textures: limestone, travertine, teak, lime plaster, linen.
  • LandscapeReal palms, trees and shrubs placed across the plot, plus the shoreline and the bay.
  • LightingRendered in Blender and baked into the model, so the walkthrough looks lit, not flat.
  • Web buildCompressed so the full estate streams into a phone or laptop browser.
