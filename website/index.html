The place

A house built around the light.

Aurelle sits between a coconut grove and an open beach. Every room is placed to catch one hour of the day well: the east pergola at nine, the pool at four, the courtyard when the frangipani throws its shade.

Travertine floors stay cool underfoot. Teak, cane and lime plaster age quietly. Glass opens fully to the sea, so the house feels like a veranda with walls added later.
