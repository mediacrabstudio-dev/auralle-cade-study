Soleva renewal

A clean serum campaign: minimal product language made tactile through water, shadow and atmospheric close-ups.

Scroll for the story
Overview

Soleva extends its beauty world into a calmer, more clinical expression while keeping its premium material feel.

Client
Soleva
Industry
Skincare
Role
Campaign direction, product imagery
Focus
30 ml renewal serum
Challenge

A calm serum story that still feels ownable in a crowded skincare feed.

Solution

A restrained palette and close crops keep the label readable while water supplies the energy.

Impact

A complete suite for product pages, social launches, carousels and paid creative.

Approach

Quiet but sensory: water droplets, warm shadows, cream labels and glass reflections create movement without crowding the product.
