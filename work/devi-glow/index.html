Overview

Devi Glow needed a premium launch system that feels luminous, direct and instantly recognisable across packaging, social and hero product moments.

Client
Devi Glow
Industry
Skincare
Role
Art direction, packaging, launch imagery
Cue
24K gold
Challenge

A beauty identity that feels rich and ritual-led without becoming quiet or predictable.

Solution

Gold glass, saturated backdrops and restrained serif type, with the product as the anchor.

Impact

A flexible launch language for product pages, reel covers, paid social and retail.

Approach

We treated the serum as an object of light: yellow fields, magenta shadows, reflective glass and close crops.
