Overview

Not every launch needs a shoot. Our film lab turns a storyboard into a finished product film in days, with the same lighting, pacing and finish as a studio commercial.

Type
Product films
Categories
Beauty, tech, automotive
Our work
Storyboard, direction, generation, edit
Format
16:9 · 9:16 cut-downs
Challenge

Brands need launch films faster and more often than a traditional shoot allows.

Solution

Storyboard-first direction, AI generation for each shot, and a human edit and grade on top.

Delivered

Seven product films across beauty, tech and automotive, with social cut-downs.

The idea

Storyboard first, then generate. Every film starts as a frame-by-frame plan, so the AI works to a director's brief, not the other way round.
