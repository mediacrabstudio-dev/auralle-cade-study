Aurea above it all

0:00

Twelve penthouses at the top of a tower: photoreal CGI, a brand film, identity, print and the launch campaign, all in midnight navy and burnished gold.

Scroll for the story
Overview

Aurea sells one thing nobody else in the city can: the view from the very top. We built the brand around that height, from the glowing crown of the tower to the private preview invitation.

Project
Aurea Penthouses
Product
12 limited penthouses
Our work
Identity, CGI, film, print, campaign
Assets
17 visuals · film · system
Challenge

Sell a handful of penthouses at the top of a tower that is not built yet, to buyers who have seen everything.

Solution

Night-lit CGI of the crown, the terrace and the homes, carried by a quiet navy-and-gold identity.

Delivered

Brand identity, a CGI library, a brand film, brochure and preview kit, sales lounge, hoarding and launch posts.

The idea

Above it all. Every frame starts from the top floor and looks down at a city that has gone quiet, so the buyer feels the height before they see the price.
