Overview

Artha's promise is simple: a home that pays you back. We built the whole world around it, from the tower at golden hour to the hoarding on the highway, in one cobalt and signal-orange voice.

Client
Artha Developers
Project
3, 4 & 5 BHK · GIFT City
Our work
Identity, CGI, film, campaign, social
Assets
24 visuals · film · system
Challenge

Make a tower that is not yet built feel real, premium and worth the investment, across every touchpoint.

Solution

Photoreal stills of the tower, the homes and the amenities, carried by a bold blue-and-orange campaign system.

Delivered

Brand identity, a CGI library, a brand film, brochure, hoardings, site wall, experience centre and the launch feed.

The idea

Walls that mean something. A home is the only investment that pays you back, so every picture shows what you get back: light, space, time together.
