Overview

Pura Vita is a gel cream made from plants, so we took the jar back into the forest: moss, waterfalls, jasmine and morning light, with the green glass jar always in focus.

Brand
Pura Vita
Product

Our work
Art direction, film, product stills
Assets

Challenge

Show freshness and hydration in a single still, without looking like every other green skincare brand.

Solution

Real-feeling nature scenes with water in motion and white flowers as the recurring accent.

Delivered

A product film, a hero library and texture grids for the product page and ads.

The idea

Bottled from the forest floor. Every frame puts the jar back where its ingredients grow, with water and white blossom doing the talking.
