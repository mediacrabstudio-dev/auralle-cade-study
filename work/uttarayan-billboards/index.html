Uttarayan, on the skyline

0:00

Festival billboards for Nagar's Uttarayan at The House of MG: a kite-filled palace scene animated for Times LED screens and a 6,144-pixel airport wall.

Scroll for the story
Overview

Uttarayan fills the sky with kites for two days. We brought that sky onto the city's screens: a palace in festival pink, kites lifting across it, and the name landing where every passer-by can read it in a glance.

Client
Nagar · The House of MG
Media
Times LED screens · airport wall
Our work
Design, 2D animation, delivery
Format
10 s loops · 2560×1080 and 6144×1728
Challenge

An outdoor screen is seen for a few seconds, from far away, often from a moving car. The message has to land at a glance.

Solution

One scene, one colour and one name. The motion is gentle and readable: kites drift, the type settles, and the loop restarts without a cut.

Delivery

Master files built to each screen's exact pixel size, so nothing is scaled or cropped on site.

01The screens

Two shapes, one festival

The same artwork rebuilt for two very different canvases: the wide city LED and the extra-wide airport wall.

02The look

Festival pink

A small palette taken from Uttarayan itself: palace pink, kite colours and a clear sky, so the screen reads from across the road.
