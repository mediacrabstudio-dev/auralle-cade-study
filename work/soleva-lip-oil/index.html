Soléva velvet lip oil

0:00

A berry-glaze lip oil launch: two product films, packaging, gifting and a twenty-frame still library in deep wine, velvet and gold.

Scroll for the story
Overview

Velvet Lip Oil is about one sensation: glaze. We shot everything in wine-dark light, with blackberries, gold and slow liquid, so every frame feels like the product looks on the lips.

Brand
Soléva
Product
Velvet Lip Oil · Berry Glaze
Our work
Packaging, films, product stills, social
Assets
2 films · 24 stills
Challenge

Make a small glass bottle feel indulgent and expensive in a feed full of lip products.

Solution

A single colour world of wine, velvet and gold, with liquid motion doing the selling.

Delivered

Two product films, packaging and gifting, a twenty-frame still library and a nine-tile launch grid.

The idea

Glaze you can almost taste. Blackberries, slow drops and deep velvet make the oil look as rich as it feels.
