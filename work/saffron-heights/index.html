An illustrated brand film for Tilak Group, made to introduce Saffron Heights, a premium residential tower in Gandhinagar.

Scroll for the story
Overview

One flower opens, and a home rises inside it. In thirty-one seconds the film turns a saffron crocus into a lamp, the lamp into a tower, and the tower into a place to stand and look out from.

Client
Tilak Group
Project
Saffron Heights, Gandhinagar
Our work
Brand film: concept, illustration, 2D animation
Length
0:31 · 16:9 and 9:16 · 2025
The idea

Saffron is rare, warm and slow to grow. We let the name lead the film: everything is drawn in the colours of the flower, and every shape grows out of the one before it.

01The film, beat by beat

Seven beats, one continuous bloom

Each beat hands its shape to the next, so the film plays as one unbroken movement, from the first petal to the Tilak Group logo.

0:00 · The budA single saffron crocus rises into frame

0:03 · The lampThe bloom opens and hangs on a wire, like a pendant light

0:06 · The lineThe lamp's wire draws the outline of the tower

0:09 · The towerThe tower fills in under saffron clouds

0:12 · The push inThe camera moves past a tree, toward a window

0:13 · InsideWe cut inside the home, looking back at the tower

0:18 · The nameSaffron Heights, with the stigma in place of the o

0:22 · The offerPremium 3 & 4 BHK

0:28 · The sign offThe Tilak Group logo

02Every second

Thirty-one seconds, one frame each

One frame from every second of the film. Read left to right, it is the whole story in a single line.

03The look

Drawn in saffron

A tight palette taken from the flower itself: apricot paper, rose petals, terracotta for the tower, and a deep night tone for the one interior scene. These swatches are sampled straight from the film.

04Process

From a name to a film

How the film came together, step by step.

01

Reading the name

We started from the word saffron: its flower, its colour and its rarity, and asked how a flower could become a building.

02

Script and beats

A single transformation, timed to thirty-one seconds: bud, lamp, line, tower, window, name, offer, sign off.

03

Styleframes

Key frames for each beat, locking the apricot palette and the soft engraved line used for the flower and the tower.

04

Illustration

The crocus, the clouds, the tower, the interior and the blossom branch, all drawn as one family.

05

Animation

2D animation with match transitions, so each shape grows out of the one before it and the film never cuts away from the idea.

06

Sound and delivery

Music and sound to the picture, then a 16:9 master and a 9:16 cut for social.

05Vertical cut

Made for the feed too

A 9:16 version of the same film, for Reels and Stories.

Format

9:16, the same 31 second edit, reframed around the flower and the tower.

The project

Saffron Heights is a single tower in Gandhinagar with premium 3 & 4 BHK homes and shops, and 60% of the site kept as open green space.

Client

Tilak Group, Gandhinagar.
